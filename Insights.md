# Olist E-Commerce Business Intelligence Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset to understand the factors affecting revenue, customer satisfaction, payment behavior, and delivery performance. Multiple datasets were combined to create a unified business intelligence dataset for analysis and insight generation.

## Dataset Information

* Dataset: Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Dataset
* Total Orders: 98,665
* Total Revenue: 20.42 Million
* Tools Used: Python, Pandas, Jupyter Notebook, Matplotlib
* Domain: E-Commerce Analytics

## Key Metrics

* Total Revenue: 20.42M
* Total Orders: 98,665
* Average Review Score: 4.03
* Late Delivery Rate: 6.53%

## Key Findings

### Revenue Analysis

* The business generated more than 20.42M in total revenue.
* Bed Bath Table was the highest revenue-generating category.
* Health Beauty and Computers Accessories were also among the top-performing categories.
* SP generated the highest revenue among all states, followed by RJ and MG.

### Customer Satisfaction Analysis

* The average customer review score was 4.03 out of 5.
* Five-star reviews represented the largest share of customer feedback.
* Customer satisfaction was strongly influenced by delivery performance.

### Delivery Performance Analysis

* The overall late delivery rate was 6.53%.
* Orders delivered on time received an average review score of 4.18.
* Late deliveries received an average review score of only 2.20.
* Delivery delays significantly reduced customer satisfaction.

### Payment Behavior Analysis

* Credit Card was the dominant payment method.
* Credit Card generated approximately 15.67M in revenue.
* More than 75,000 orders were completed using Credit Card payments.
* Boleto was the second most commonly used payment method.

### Regional Analysis

* SP was the strongest revenue-generating state.
* AL recorded the highest late delivery rate.
* Delivery performance varied across different states.

## Business Insights

1. Delivery performance has a direct impact on customer satisfaction.
2. Reducing late deliveries can significantly improve review scores.
3. Credit Card payments drive the majority of business revenue.
4. Revenue is concentrated in a few key product categories.
5. SP remains the most important market for revenue generation.
6. High-delay states should be prioritized for logistics improvements.

## Business Recommendations

1. Improve logistics operations to reduce delivery delays.
2. Monitor high-risk regions with elevated late-delivery rates.
3. Focus inventory planning on top-performing categories.
4. Enhance customer experience through faster delivery services.
5. Continue optimizing payment processes around Credit Card users.
6. Develop targeted strategies for high-revenue states.
